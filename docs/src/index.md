## 📜 The JamLiquor Manifesto

- **Vision**: A world where blockchain runs everywhere, from $10 boards to data centers
- **Mission**: Prove — not assume — that a minimal, conformant JAM core is lightweight enough for constrained hardware
- **Values**: Two claims to be earned via CI evidence, not marketing
- **Roadmap**: MVP-1 (conformance) → MVP-2/3 (edge benchmarks), with pre-committed stop conditions

[Read the full Manifesto →](./MANIFESTO.md)

## 🌟 Core Claims

Exactly two claims remain in current scope, each gated by a milestone in [`spec.md`](../../spec.md) — both are hypotheses under test, not settled facts:

1. 🚀 **Lightweight** — gated by MVP-2/3 edge benchmarks (due 2027-02-15). Zero measurements exist as of 2026-08. See [Lightweight by Design](./EDGE.md) for the pre-pivot vision doc this claim narrows down from.
2. 🌐 **JAM-native** — gated by MVP-1 conformance (due 2026-11-15): binary codec + Safrole STF provably agreeing with the official test vectors, not just containing JAM-related code. See [Simplicity Is Strength](./ARCHITECTURE.md) for the pre-pivot design philosophy; the current concrete plan is `jam-core`'s modules (`spec.md` §6).

Retired from the identity: "Real Decentralization" (networking is out of current scope, see [Decentralization vision](./DECENTRALIZATION.md), pre-pivot), "Adaptability Through Inference" (AI), and "Quantum Resistance" (PQC) — the latter two are covered in [Research Notes](./research-notes.md).
